The Problem This Solves — What to Tell Madam

The Scale of India's Air Crisis

India has 14 of the world's 20 most polluted cities (IQAir, 2024). Air pollution causes an estimated 7 million deaths globally per year (WHO). Cities regularly exceed AQI 150 — "Unhealthy" — and hit 300+ around Diwali and winters.

The Information Gap

The problem is not that data doesn't exist. The problem is that a person sees AQI = 187 — a raw number with zero context. They don't know:

  • Is 187 dangerous for me specifically (asthma, child, elderly)?
  • Will it get better or worse in the next 6 hours?
  • Should I let my child go to school tomorrow?

Existing apps show numbers. They don't explain, don't forecast, and don't advise.

What AirSense Does Instead

1
Live color-coded gaugeNot just a number — "Unhealthy · avoid outdoor activity"
2
48-hour forecast chartWill tomorrow be better or worse?
3
Interactive India mapSee pollution hotspots across cities
4
Private AI health chatType: "I have asthma, can I jog tomorrow?" → cited, grounded answer
5
Telegram phone alertsNotify you before you step outside into bad air

Everything runs on the laptop. Questions never reach the cloud. The AI never pretends to be a doctor.

Three Honest Risks

⚡
Risk 1 — Kafka setup takes 2–3 hours on Day 1
Windows path-length errors are real. Fix: install to C:\kafka. Use Java 17 in JAVA_HOME. After first-time setup it just works. This is normal. Everyone fights it.
📦
Risk 2 — LangChain version sensitivity
Pin every library version in requirements.txt from Day 1. Don't pip install latest blindly or you'll get import errors when something changes upstream.
📈
Risk 3 — Model RMSE may not hit target immediately
Target is RMSE < 30 AQI units. You may get 35–40 in early weeks. That's fine — document it, improve features, iterate. Honesty in defense is better than inflated claims.

One-Paragraph Summary — For Report / Madam
AirSense is a real-time air quality intelligence system built entirely on a single laptop with zero cloud dependency and zero cost. It ingests live AQI data from 7+ Indian cities every hour through an Apache Kafka streaming pipeline, validates and stores readings in a TimescaleDB time-series database, trains a LightGBM machine learning model weekly to forecast pollution levels 24–48 hours ahead, and delivers insights through a Streamlit dashboard featuring live color-coded gauges, an interactive India map, and a 48-hour forecast chart. Its centrepiece is a private generative AI health advisor — powered by a local LLM (Ollama + phi3:mini) and a RAG pipeline built on official WHO and CPCB health guidelines — that answers personal health questions like "Is it safe for my asthmatic child to go to school tomorrow?" with cited, grounded, non-hallucinated answers, entirely on-device. A Telegram alert bot notifies the user when AQI spikes, and automated MLOps ensures the forecasting model improves every week without manual intervention. AirSense covers the complete spectrum of modern data roles — data engineering, machine learning, generative AI, MLOps, BI reporting, and software engineering — all in one integrated, production-grade, demo-ready system.
